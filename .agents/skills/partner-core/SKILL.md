# Partner core — re-anchor without steering

Use after a long investigation, stale-context recovery or a consequential disagreement. Loading this skill is not a new task or permission to change the objective. Resume from current evidence and user intent, not an old plan or this reminder itself.

## Recover the governing frame

The [system contract](../../../config/APPEND_SYSTEM.md) owns baseline behavior. Revisit its [working frame](../../../config/APPEND_SYSTEM.md#1-frame-the-task-before-acting) when intent or authority is unclear, and its [recovery guidance](../../../config/APPEND_SYSTEM.md#recover-cheaply) when repeated corrections share a failed premise. Do not recite a checklist before each tool call.

For testing your interpretation of the goal, proactive reframing, outcome-based decomposition or continuity, use [goal-management](../../../extensions/guidepin/skills/goal-management/SKILL.md). It owns the deeper workflow, templates and scaffolder. Re-anchoring is not itself a request to read or update goal files; this skill does not maintain another goal procedure.

- Assess independently; agreement, familiarity and agent consensus are not evidence.
- Keep investigation and proposals broad while respecting the actual action boundary.
- Challenge the premise when evidence defeats the approach; do not stack another patch on it.
- Put evidence and its limits beside consequential claims. Keep the final handoff independently understandable.

## Runtime ownership

[guidepin](../../../extensions/guidepin/README.md) is a standalone development extension, not enabled by the default aggregate. Its [index.ts](../../../extensions/guidepin/index.ts) owns the partner lens and hook wiring; [append-salience.ts](../../../extensions/guidepin/append-salience.ts) owns `GOAL_REMINDER`, capsule assembly and cadence. Both reminder paths reuse that compact goal wording; this skill is not a copy of either payload.

Hook semantics and historical transport evidence belong to [pi-hooks-and-design.md](../../../extensions/guidepin/docs/pi-hooks-and-design.md). There is no `/append-remind` command, proposed hook registry, or requirement to narrate a goal before every tool. Applying versioned prompt defaults to the host remains the confirmed `jeito-setup` flow.
